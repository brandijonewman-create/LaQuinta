═══════════════════════════════════════════
CALIFORNIA-SPECIFIC EDITORIAL CONSIDERATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════
1. California has materially higher state income tax than Arizona. Many Coachella Valley golf-community buyers are California residents trading down from coastal-California pricing; they are NOT typically seeking a tax-residency move. The editorial angle should reflect this — Coachella Valley is a lifestyle play, not a tax play.
2. California prop-tax structure (Prop 13) is unusual and worth a dedicated editorial guide. Newer purchasers face higher effective tax than long-time owners due to base-year value assessments.
3. The Coachella Valley is genuinely seasonal — many private clubs close or reduce operations in summer. Acknowledge this honestly.
4. Water-rights and drought considerations are legitimate ongoing news in Coachella Valley. Address them editorially without alarmism.
5. The aging-in-place demographic is meaningful — Sun City Palm Desert, The Springs, and several other communities target the 55+ buyer specifically.
