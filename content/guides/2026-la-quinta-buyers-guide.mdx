## The seven clubs at a glance

- **The Madison Club** &mdash; Tom Fazio. Most discreet, most luxury-tilted.
- **The Hideaway** &mdash; Pete Dye + Clive Clark. Two courses, one gate.
- **PGA West** &mdash; Six courses, four architects. Widest price range.
- **The Tradition Golf Club** &mdash; Arnold Palmer's only Coachella Valley design.
- **Andalusia Country Club** &mdash; Rees Jones. Newer, modern Spanish architecture.
- **The Quarry at La Quinta** &mdash; Tom Fazio. Built on a former rock quarry.
- **La Quinta Country Club** &mdash; The original. Founded 1959.

## The California-specific context

Three things that distinguish a La Quinta purchase from an Arizona one:

1. **State income tax.** California's top marginal rate is materially higher than Arizona's. Most La Quinta buyers are *not* relocating their tax residency; they're trading down from coastal-California pricing. Treat La Quinta as a lifestyle decision, not a tax move.
2. **Prop 13 reassessment.** New buyers face a base-year reassessment that often shocks first-timers. The seller's property tax bill is not your property tax bill.
3. **Seasonality.** The valley is genuinely a winter market. Plan your usage accordingly.
