## How to read this guide

Club-by-club differences in La Quinta matter more than buyers expect. Two homes at the same price in two different clubs can produce a 30–40% difference in total annual cost of ownership once dues, capital contributions, HOA, and Mello-Roos are factored in. Architectural fit, course style, and member culture create another layer of difference buyers don't see until after they've moved in.

## The Madison Club
- **Architect:** Tom Fazio · **Courses:** 1 private 18 · **Membership:** Highly restricted, invitation-driven culture.
- **Buyer profile:** Second- or third-home buyer at the top of the market. Privacy-forward.

## PGA West
- **Architects:** Pete Dye, Jack Nicklaus, Greg Norman, Tom Weiskopf · **Courses:** 6
- **Membership:** Multiple tiers — Resort, Stadium, Private. Each tier has distinct access and pricing.
- **Buyer profile:** Widest in the valley — from condo buyers in the $500Ks to estate buyers at $5M+.

## The Hideaway
- **Architects:** Pete Dye, Clive Clark · **Courses:** 2 private 18s · **Membership:** Equity, tight roster.
- **Buyer profile:** Architecturally-engaged buyer who values uniformity and quiet.

## Andalusia Country Club
- **Architect:** Rees Jones · **Courses:** 1 · **Membership:** Equity. Publicly visible initiation and dues structure.
- **Buyer profile:** Newer-construction buyer at the southern edge of La Quinta.

## The Tradition Golf Club
- **Architect:** Arnold Palmer · **Courses:** 1 · **Membership:** Equity, premium-tier.
- **Buyer profile:** Architecture- and design-focused. Often a Palmer-design enthusiast.

## La Quinta Country Club
- **Architects:** Lawrence & Frank Hughes (1959) · **Membership:** Equity, member-owned since 1977.
- **Buyer profile:** Heritage-oriented. Walking distance to the historic resort and the Cove.

## The Quarry at La Quinta
- **Architect:** Tom Fazio · **Membership:** Equity, capped at fewer than 250 members.
- **Buyer profile:** Discreet ultra-luxury buyer who prefers smallness over recognition.

## What this comparison can't capture

Member culture, kitchen culture, the practice of letting friends play through, the depth of the dining program, the pickleball court politics, the New Year's Eve party. These are real and they vary. The only honest way to assess fit is to stay in each community for a week, ideally during high season. The PDF copy includes a printable site-visit checklist.
