La Quinta is the densest concentration of championship private golf in California. Seven private clubs sit inside the city limits, designed by seven of the most influential names in modern course architecture &mdash; Pete Dye, Tom Fazio, Jack Nicklaus, Greg Norman, Arnold Palmer, Tom Weiskopf, and Rees Jones.

This site exists to cover that, honestly.

## What this site is

The definitive guide to:

- The seven private clubs
- The architects behind their fairways
- The lifestyle that surrounds them
- The honest economics of buying into a La Quinta golf community

## What this site isn't

Not a brokerage. Not a listings portal (yet). Not the place where you'll read invented quotes from "Sarah K., happy buyer" or fabricated median price figures. Every market range we publish gets a disclaimer asking you to verify with a licensed California real-estate professional before you transact.

## What's coming

Full profiles of all seven clubs, dedicated architect pages, a 2026 La Quinta Buyer's Guide, an honest market report for the 92253 ZIP, and an interactive lifestyle map. The IDX listings page goes live the day a verified California feed and a partner California-licensed agent are in place.

Until then, the listings page will tell you the truth: coming soon.
