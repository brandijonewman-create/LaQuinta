La Quinta Country Club is the oldest private club in the city of La Quinta and one of the oldest in the Coachella Valley. It opened in 1959 with a Lawrence Hughes routing and grew alongside the La Quinta Resort and the historic Cove neighborhood. The defining qualities of the club today are its location — walking distance to the Cove, Old Town, and the Resort — and the mature streetscape that surrounds it. The membership skews more multi-generational than at the newer La Quinta clubs, and the architectural fabric of the surrounding neighborhood is a mix of mid-century, classic Spanish, and more recent custom remodels.

This guide covers what a serious buyer should understand before a visit — the course, how membership and access work, the varied real estate, how the club compares to its neighbors, and the questions worth asking on a tour. La Quinta Golf Lifestyle profiles La Quinta's private golf communities across the valley, and La Quinta Country Club is where the city's golf history began.

## The Lawrence Hughes course

The Hughes routing is a classic mid-century traditional design — modest elevation changes, tree-lined corridors that have matured into one of the most visually settled member experiences in La Quinta, and green complexes that reward conservative second shots. The course has been periodically refreshed, but the underlying routing is original. It is shorter than most of the newer La Quinta courses, which is a feature for many members and a drawback for those who want championship-grade length.

## Membership and access at La Quinta Country Club

La Quinta Country Club has historically been more family-multigenerational than most of the newer La Quinta privates, and the social calendar is correspondingly active. It is a member-and-guest club with no resort play.

## Homes at La Quinta Country Club: what buyers should know

The streetscape around La Quinta Country Club is the most architecturally varied of the La Quinta clubs. Mid-century single-story originals sit next to classic Spanish-Mediterranean homes from the 1970s and 80s and to recent custom remodels. Mature trees and irrigated landscaping are dominant. Original mid-century single-family is the most accessible price entry point in La Quinta private-club real estate, and the club has the largest active resale market of the La Quinta privates. Specific pricing should be confirmed with a local specialist against current listings.

## How La Quinta Country Club compares

Among the La Quinta privates, La Quinta Country Club is the historic, walkable, multi-generational option — the opposite end of the spectrum from the newer, gated, southern clubs like Madison and Andalusia. Its proximity to the Cove and Old Town gives it a walkability that none of the southern La Quinta clubs share, a meaningful lifestyle differentiator. ## Your pre-tour checklist

Every serious buyer should have answers to the following before or during a visit:

- Ask about original vs. remodel scope — the community's inventory is a wide mix.
- Check permitting and setback for any planned addition; the older layout has tighter setbacks than the newer La Quinta clubs.
- Verify the HOA structure by sub-neighborhood — the surrounding neighborhood is a patchwork.
- Walk the neighborhood to Old Town — walkability is a signature differentiator here.
- Confirm current initiation, dues, and availability directly with the membership office.

## Is La Quinta Country Club the right fit for you?

La Quinta Country Club is the best fit for a multi-generational buyer who wants an active social calendar, walkable neighborhood context, and a mature streetscape rather than newer construction. It is less the answer for a buyer set on championship length, the strictest modern architectural code, or brand-new estate product.
