# SEO Playbooks Index — Scottsdale Golf Lifestyle

These playbooks are the off-site SEO action plan for Scottsdale Golf Lifestyle. On-site SEO is already built (100+ pages, full JSON-LD schema, FAQ rich snippets on every community page, dynamic OG images, SPA page_view tracking, internal cross-linking). What remains is **off-site authority building** — earned backlinks, journalist relationships, and editorial syndication.

## Site framing (read first)
Scottsdale Golf Lifestyle is an **independent editorial publication**, not a real-estate brokerage. The owner and creator is Brandi Jo Newman, referenced only as "owner and creator" — never as agent, broker, or licensed real-estate professional. The site supports itself through display advertising, the gated lead-magnet system, and partnerships. **This positioning shapes everything below.** Strategies designed for agent marketing sites (Google Business Profile with a physical office, traditional NAP citation building, Realtor.com / Zillow Premier Agent listings) do not apply and have been deliberately removed from this playbook set.

## Files

1. **01-backlink-outreach.md** — How to earn quality backlinks for an independent editorial publication: HARO/Connectively responses, local journalist outreach, golf-publication guest posts, resource-page placement, broken-link building, and embeddable widgets. The single biggest ranking factor in Google's algorithm.

## Suggested 90-day plan

**Weeks 1-2**: Foundation
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap
- Pull a current backlink baseline in Ahrefs or Moz free trial; document it
- Apply to Google Publisher Center (eligible because we publish original editorial)
- Sign up for HARO/Connectively and Qwoted; set up daily digests

**Weeks 3-6**: Earned media
- Respond to 3 relevant HARO queries per week with the editorial-publisher response template (see 01-backlink-outreach.md)
- Research and email 4 local journalists at AZ outlets (Arizona Republic, AZ Big Media, Phoenix Business Journal, Scottsdale Progress) with quarterly market-report pitches
- Identify 10 resource pages that would plausibly link to a Scottsdale-real-estate editorial guide; email each owner

**Weeks 7-12**: Compounding
- Pitch 2-3 guest editorial pieces to Golf.com, GolfDigest.com, Links Magazine, AZ Foothills
- Begin broken-link building outreach
- Add reasonable Wikipedia citations to articles where our content is the most authoritative public source
- Consider building one embeddable widget (community comparison or market snapshot) for passive backlink generation

## Suggested execution model

A skilled marketing assistant or freelance journalist-outreach contractor at $25-50/hour can execute the editorial-outreach work in roughly 20-30 hours/month. The work is research-heavy and relationship-heavy; lower-priced VA labor generally underperforms here because the response quality and personalization on HARO/journalist pitches directly determines whether you earn the citation.

## How to know it's working

Monthly KPIs to track:
- Referring domains (Ahrefs/Moz/Ubersuggest)
- High-authority referring domains (DA 50+)
- Featured in journalist outlets — count direct citations and backlinks per quarter
- Resource-page placements
- Editorial syndications or republications
- Organic search traffic and keyword ranking trends (GA4 + Search Console)

**Realistic 6-month goal**: 30+ quality referring domains, 8+ journalist citations, 15+ resource-page placements. **No specific Domain Authority target** — DA is a Moz proprietary metric and not a Google ranking factor; track it as a directional signal only.
